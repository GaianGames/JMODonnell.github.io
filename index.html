As a writer, editor, and worldbuilder, I specialize in story-driven projects spanning games, memoir, and prose fiction.

I hold a first-class honours degree in English, Philosophy, and Creative Writing from the University of Galway. There, under the tutelage of writers such as Mike McCormack, Alan McMonagle, and Nicole Flattery, I combined philosophical study, an in-depth exploration of literary short fcition, and a love of magical realism, to form my own unique voice inspired by character and question-driven narrative. In 2020, my work "The Circle, The Sickle, The Star" was shortlisted by Globe Soup.

Currently, I act as an occassional Worldbuilding and Narrative Consultant for Soul Atlas, a cross-media fantasy project in development. I have previously worked with Soul Atlas as a Worldbuilder & Lore Keeper where I developed character profiles and backstories, and co-developed comprehensive narrative bibles covering geopolitics, factional histories and ideologies, and speculative philosophies. I further collaborated across departments to ensure canonical consistency, to advise on animated trailer scripts, and participate in writers’ rooms to deepen narrative cohesion.

To studios and collaborators, I offer a comprehesive understanding of literary prose fiction and its mechanics; my experience as a Writer and Worldbuilder with a ground-up worldbuilding approach with no aversion to hard worldbuidling and first principles design; and a passion for story-driven projects.

Outside the game industry, I’ve led editorial teams as the Managing Editor at Inspire Me Korea, a print and digital cultural magazine with a subscriber base that spanned over 43 countries. I’m also a published ghostwriter and the Founder of the Galway Writing Collective, a grassroots initiative supporting emerging writers through regular workshops and community events.

My writing background includes fiction writing, short-form prose, non-fiction, cultural writing, and academic research and investigative writing in the field of information ethics. Whether I’m constructing fictional timelines, editing manuscripts, or developing narrative frameworks, I bring an adaptive, research-driven, and collaborative approach to every project.

Looking for a writer, editor, or narrative consultant? I'm available for freelance and contract work across narrative games, transmedia storytelling, editorial development, and more. Let’s build something remarkable.

Founding the Collective

When I arrived in Galway in 2019, a prose collective didn't exist, and I suffered under the common belief that writing is something that has to be done in isolation. However, I wanted to change this, and when I couldn't find a collective, I decided to form one.

The Galway Writing Collective was born out of a desire to give a space to prose writers and to develop a sense of community. I wanted to help foster a sense of connection and nurture the arts in Galway while bringing people together.

Through dedication and strategic vision, I have successfully nurtured and supported writers of various levels by leading a weekly prose workshop designed to foster growth and creativity. I crafted a brand that has garnered widespread recognition, building a public following that includes literary journals, publishers, and authors. My expertise in event management is showcased in my ability to facilitate four monthly events, seamlessly liaising with venues and overseeing all aspects of coordination. Additionally, I established and led Galway’s only regular technical editing workshop, providing a valuable resource for writers seeking to refine their craft.

The group is now on hiatus, but discussions are underway to return in a more sustainable way.

J.M O'Donnell, Founder of The Galway Writing Collective.
