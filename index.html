About Me

A Brief Introduction

Writer. Editor. Worldbuilder. I specialize in story-driven projects that span games, fiction, and editorial content.

I hold a first-class honours degree in English, Philosophy, and Creative Writing from the University of Galway, where I developed a strong foundation in literary craft, narrative design, and philosophical storytelling. My work bridges the literary and interactive—offering studios and collaborators a unique blend of traditional storytelling expertise and worldbuilding precision.

Currently, I’m the Writer & Lorekeeper at Soul Atlas, a cross-media fantasy project in development. There, I develop comprehensive narrative bibles covering geopolitics, factional histories, and speculative philosophies. I collaborate across departments to ensure canonical consistency, advise on animated trailer scripts, and participate in writers’ rooms to deepen narrative cohesion.

Outside the game industry, I’ve led editorial teams as the Managing Editor at Inspire Me Korea, a print and digital cultural magazine with a global subscriber base. I’m also a published ghostwriter and the Founder of the Galway Writing Collective, a grassroots initiative supporting emerging writers through regular workshops and community events.

My background includes fiction writing, short-form prose, non-fiction, cultural journalism, and research on ethics in digital design. Whether I’m constructing fictional timelines, editing manuscripts, or developing voice and tone for narrative systems, I bring an adaptive, research-driven, and collaborative approach to every project.

Looking for a writer, editor, or narrative consultant? I'm available for freelance and contract work across narrative games, transmedia storytelling, editorial development, and more. Let’s build something remarkable.

Founding the Collective

When I arrived in Galway in 2019, a prose collective didn't exist, and I suffered under the common belief that writing is something that has to be done in isolation. However, I wanted to change this, and when I couldn't find a collective, I decided to form one.

The Galway Writing Collective was born out of a desire to give a space to prose writers and to develop a sense of community. I wanted to help foster a sense of connection and nurture the arts in Galway while bringing people together.

  • A weekly prose workshop to nurture and support writers of various levels.
  • Crafted a brand and built a public following including literary journals, publishers, and authors.
  • Facilitated four monthly events, liaised with venues, and oversaw event management.
  • Ran Galway’s only regular technical editing workshop once a month.

The group is now on hiatus, but discussions are underway to return in a more sustainable way.

J.M O'Donnell, Founder of The Galway Writing Collective.
