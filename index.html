About Me

Writer & Worldbuilder | Musician | Narrative Designer | Editor

I craft stories, shape narratives, and build worlds across games, music, and fiction.

I graduated with first-class honours in English, Philosophy, and Creative Writing, mentored by internationally acclaimed voices including Mike McCormack, Alan McMonagle, and Nicole Flattery. Under their guidance, I combined a love of magical realism with an architectural understanding of story structure, voice, immersion, and characterization. My work embodies memory, identity, and the spaces between things in the pursuit of perspective and the healing of our social fabric.

In 2020, my work The Circle, The Sickle, The Star was shortlisted for the Globe Soup Prize. Since then, I have grown my craft across genres and cross-media IP: from developing character bibles and branching narrative flows for story-driven games, to collaborating as Managing Editor at Inspire Me Korea — a cultural magazine bridging global stories, identity, and cultural insight.

I have previously served as worldbuilder, lore-keeper, and editor for Soul Atlas, an ambitious cross-media fantasy project where I crafted and combined geopolitics, culture, ideology, and philosophy together with character and topography to co-create an original, cohesive universe. I continue to serve as an occasional narrative and worldbuilding consultant.

As the Founder of the Galway Writing Collective, and now co-founder of the Galway Writers' Community, I cultivate new literary voices and foster community among prose writers in the West of Ireland. We are a grassroots initiative supporting emerging writers through regular workshops and community events — reflecting an unwavering commitment to the importance of human connection, to play, and to narrative craft.

I am now available for freelance, full-time, and consulting projects in narrative design, transmedia storytelling, editing, and worldbuilding. If you're seeking a partner in building new realms — or breathing new life into existing ones — I'd love to connect.

Founding the Collective

When I arrived in Galway in 2019, a prose collective didn't exist, and I suffered under the common belief that writing is something that has to be done in isolation. However, I wanted to change this, and when I couldn't find a collective, I decided to form one.

The Galway Writing Collective was born out of a desire to give a space to prose writers and to develop a sense of community. I wanted to help foster a sense of connection and nurture the arts in Galway while bringing people together.

Through dedication and strategic vision, I have successfully nurtured and supported writers of various levels by leading a weekly prose workshop designed to foster growth and creativity. I crafted a brand that has garnered widespread recognition, building a public following that includes literary journals, publishers, and authors. My expertise in event management is showcased in my ability to facilitate four monthly events, seamlessly liaising with venues and overseeing all aspects of coordination. Additionally, I established and led Galway’s only regular technical editing workshop, providing a valuable resource for writers seeking to refine their craft.

The group is now on hiatus, but discussions are underway to return in a more sustainable way.

J.M O'Donnell, Founder of The Galway Writing Collective.
